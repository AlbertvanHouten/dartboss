# Games and options

Every game can be played against friends on the same device, and most against bots too. Any game with two or more players can also be played across phones: each player uses their own phone, and the host can score for players without one. See [Players on their own phones](#players-on-their-own-phones).

**Matches**, against bots or friends on the same device:

- **X01** (1–8 players):
  - Start score 301, 501, 701 or any custom score.
  - Straight, double or master in, and straight, double or master out. Master means a double or treble.
  - Legs or sets, played first to or best of, with an optional two-clear rule.
  - Player 1 or a random player throws first.
  - Shows a checkout route for every finishable score.
- **Cricket** (2 players): 15–20 and the bull, with marks per round.
- **Killer** (2–8 players): random numbers and 3 lives. Hit your own double to become a killer.
- **Shanghai** (1–8 players):
  - Numbers 1–7 or 1–20. A single, double and treble in one visit wins outright.
  - Optional "require number" (miss the number and your score halves) or "require treble" (no treble and your score resets).
- **Halve It** (1–8 players):
  - The standard 12 rounds: numbers, any double, any treble, different colours, same colour, an exact total, and the bull. Or the classic 9-round version, or random order.
  - Miss a target and your score halves.

**Practice**, with personal bests per setting:

- **121 Checkout:**
  - Start target, darts per attempt (3–15), how much the target goes up, and 10, 25 or unlimited rounds.
  - Safehouse rule: what score a miss sends you back to.
- **Around the Clock** (1–8 players, bots allowed):
  - Which segments count, hits needed per target, and which bull counts.
  - Doubles and trebles can skip ahead.
  - Low to high, high to low, or random order.
- **Bob's 27** (1–8 players, bots allowed): hard mode (out below zero) or easy mode (play on).
- **Doubles Training:**
  - Stay on each double until you hit it, or throw three darts at each.
  - With or without the bull, in any order.
- **Singles Training:** any order.
- **Score Training:** 10, 20 or 33 visits, or unlimited.

**Players on their own phones**, in any game with two or more players:

- The host switches on "Players on their own phones" in the game setup. After Start, a room panel shows a 6-character code, a QR code and a join link.
- Guests tap "Join a game" in the main menu and type the code, or scan the QR code or open the link. The link opens in the browser, so the app needn't be installed. Then they tap their names to claim seats.
- On your turn your phone shows the keypad; otherwise it shows who is up. The host can score for anyone. A guest can undo their own last visit until the next player's turn is complete; after that, only the host can.
- Phones connect directly. Some guest Wi-Fi and mobile networks block that: use the host's hotspot, or let the host score for that player.
