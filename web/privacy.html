Dartboss. Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

The short version

Dartboss does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The game in progress, so you can pick it up again.
  • Your personal bests in the practice games.
  • Your match history and lifetime player stats.
  • Your app settings.
  • The player names you type in.

This is stored only on your own device. It never leaves it, except in a shared game (see below). Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes it.

Network access

The app works fully offline. The website version downloads the game files from its host when you first open it, like any website; the app itself makes no other requests, except when someone starts or joins a shared game (see below). Fonts are included with the app rather than loaded from other services.

Playing on several phones

In a shared game, each player uses their own phone. Player names and scores go only to the phones in the room, over an encrypted direct link (WebRTC). Nothing goes to the developer.

To connect, the phones exchange connection details, including IP addresses, through public Nostr relays (encrypted with the room code) and Google's public STUN servers. Neither the developer nor any server of the developer's receives or stores anything.

The app connects only when someone starts or joins a shared game. Solo and single-phone play stay fully offline.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
