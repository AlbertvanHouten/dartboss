Oche. Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

Oche Scorer does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The game in progress, so you can pick it up again.
  • Your personal bests in the practice games.
  • Your match history and lifetime player stats.
  • Your app settings.
  • The player names you type in.

This is stored only on your own device. It never leaves it, and deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes it.

Network access

The app works fully offline. The website version downloads the game files from its host when you first open it, like any website; the app itself makes no other requests. Fonts are included with the app rather than loaded from other services.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
