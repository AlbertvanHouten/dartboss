Oche. Support

Help with Oche Scorer, the darts scorer and trainer.

Which games are included?

X01 (301, 501 and 701) with checkout routes for every finishable score, plus Cricket, Killer, Shanghai and Halve It. For practice there are 121 Checkout, Around the Clock, Bob's 27, Doubles, Singles and Score Training.

How do I enter scores?

Type your visit total on the keypad and tap Score. Quick scores give you common visits in one tap. Tap the checkout button when you finish a leg on a double. Made a mistake? Undo takes back the last visit.

How do the bots work?

Set a bot to any average from 20 to 120. Bots throw at a regulation board and miss like real players do. Lower-level bots find the doubles harder, so they take longer to close out a leg.

Where are my match history and stats kept?

Only on this device. Your match history and lifetime player stats are never uploaded anywhere.

How do I reset all my data?

Go to Settings, then Reset all data. This clears your games, history, stats and personal bests. Deleting the app also deletes everything.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, Oche Scorer works without a connection.

Will the screen stay awake during a match?

Yes, so it does not go dark between visits. You can turn this off in Settings.

Do I need an account? Are there ads or tracking?

No accounts, no ads and no tracking.
